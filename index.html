Quantum Information Science & Engineering

Syed Muhammad Abuzar Rizvi

Ph.D. Candidate · Graduate Research Associate

CQILAB, Department of Electronics and Information Convergence Engineering, Kyung Hee University, South Korea

Quantum Computing  |  Quantum Machine Learning  |  Hybrid Quantum–Classical Algorithms  |  Quantum Communication

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Kyung Hee University, where I work as a Graduate Research Associate in CQILAB. My research develops machine-learning-based methods for quantum state classification and error mitigation on noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices, variational quantum algorithms for optimization in next-generation intelligent networks, reinforcement-learning-based quantum control, and protocols for reliable, privacy-preserving quantum communication.

Before my doctoral studies I received a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from NUST and worked as a DSP Engineer at NUST’s Microwave Engineering Research Lab, building FPGA-based real-time signal-processing systems.

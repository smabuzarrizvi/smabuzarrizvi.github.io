Quantum information science & engineering

Syed Muhammad Abuzar Rizvi

Ph.D. Candidate · Graduate Research Associate

CQILAB, Department of Electronics and Information Convergence Engineering, Kyung Hee University, South Korea

Learning-enhanced quantum control, variational quantum algorithms, and secure quantum networks.

My research connects quantum information with the networks and machines that will have to use it. I design learning-based and variational methods that make noisy quantum hardware more useful, from state classification and error mitigation to pulse-level control of Rydberg, single-photon and superconducting systems. I also work on quantum-communication protocols that stay reliable and private over lossy, correlated and adversarial channels.

My papers appear in IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering, IEEE Communications Letters, ACM Computing Surveys, Scientific Reports, Sensors and Mathematics.
